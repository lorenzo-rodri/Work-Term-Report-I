Abstract & Introduction

Welcome to my Co-op Work Term Report. This site highlights my key contributions, real-world learning experiences, and professional growth during my Summer 2026 co-op work term.

Since May 2026, I have been working as an IT Service Desk Co-op student at Aurora Cannabis. Throughout this term, I expanded my hardware and software support capabilities, learned enterprise IT management practices, and contributed to company-wide infrastructure deployments that make a real impact on the company and its employees. While my core position focused on ticket management and resolution, hardware provisioning, and employee onboarding/offboarding, the flexibility of the role allowed me to take on a variety of responsibilities. I used platforms like ConnectWise Automate and PowerShell to write automation scripts, saving company time and streamlining workflows. Additionally, I utilized Microsoft Azure, Intune, Exchange Online Admin, and Active Directory to manage employee accounts and devices, deploy software to computers, and manage email flows. This report covers my technical contributions, project work, and personal reflections from my time so far at Aurora.

Employer Overview

Aurora Cannabis is a global leader in the medicinal cannabis industry, with thousands of employees spread across 7 different countries. Advanced research and scientific innovation drives the company to develop a diverse medical portfolio focusing on strain-specific and blended formulations offered in THC, CBD, and balanced ratios.

At Aurora, computing and IT infrastructure power everything from the computers our scientists use to develop cutting edge strains, to the systems that manage our global supply chain and ensure data security.

Hardware & Endpoint Provisioning

Managing the lifecycle of employee hardware, provisioning laptops, desktops, and workstations configured for staff across the company. Various scripting languages and platforms are used to automate software installation, configuration, and account management for employees.

Supply Chain & Enterprise Systems

Enterprise resource planning (ERP) systems enable real-time inventory tracking, global supply chain logistics, and strict SOX compliance reporting. Sage X3 is the primary ERP system used to manage inventory, production, and distribution across multiple international locations.

Infrastructure, Servers & Storage

Maintaining enterprise IT equipment including NetApp storage clusters, physical/virtual servers, and secure network infrastructure connecting international locations. These systems allow Aurora to manage and secure sensitive data, host internal applications, and provide remote access to employees across the globe.

Work Term Goals

These learning objectives were set at the start of the term and have been evaluated upon completion:

Completed

Technical Proficiency in ConnectWise Automate and ScreenConnect

Goal: Complete ConnectWise university training and practice using the software in order to become proficient in remote device management and analyzing computer information before reaching out to users.

I completed ConnectWise training and used connectwise to troubleshoot issue with users onm a daily basis. This not only improved my problem-solving skills but also enhanced my ability to provide efficient support. I also developed a variety of scripts to automate routine tasks:

  • I resolved an issue with our FSM Log Agent installation script by creating a script that executes silent PowerShell installation steps without requiring user UI interaction.
  • I also updated a Sage Enterprise Intelligence (SEI) installation script that dynamically detects a user's OneDrive path via PowerShell to place required LabTech configuration files correctly.
  • Additionally, I built an Azure application package to automate Adobe Acrobat Pro upgrades, ensuring legacy Acrobat installations are stripped and replaced smoothly upon user sign-in.
  • Finally, I reverse-engineered an undocumented vendor VBScript used for Sage X3 reporting, converting it into a structured PowerShell script that reformats .xlsx files into specific .txt data files (YGAS/ZGAS formats) for Sage X3 imports.

ServiceNow Ticket Management and Documentation

Goal: Effectively use the ServiceNow platform to create, manage and resolve IT incident tasks and service tasks.

I developed an efficient workflow within ServiceNow to ensure urgent tickets were prioritized, and in progress tickets were resolved in a timely manner:

  • Began each shift reviewing the main queue for urgent tickets
  • Worked through and followed up with my in-progress tickets
  • Processed pending software access requests for various systems (Sage X3, SEI, CoPilot, etc.)
  • Monitored incoming requests while working on automation scripts

I also created several Knowledge Base (KB) articles documenting standard procedures, including autopilot laptop provisioning steps, and software installation steps to assist future onboarding team members.

Hardware/Software Troubleshooting

Goal: I will develop a structured IT troubleshooting process to solve technical tickets independently by the end of my co-op term, reducing reliance on senior team members.

I used a six-step diagnostic process for each technical issue that I came across, isolating variables by changing one setting at a time rather than relying on trial and error. Key outcomes of this approach included:

  • Maintained detailed escalation notes to ensure thorough documentation across ticket handoffs
  • Shadowed senior team members to accelerate technical proficiency and workflow learning
  • Built confidence to handle incoming support tickets independently, cutting down reliance on senior staff
  • Kept ticket queues clean and resolved issues efficiently

Job Description & Projects

An overview of core development tasks, operational responsibilities, and technologies used daily.

My daily responsibilities centered around ticket resolution, endpoint troubleshooting, and workspace provisioning, alongside scripting work and infrastructure maintenance. A unique aspect of this role was constructing custom desktop workstations for site security systems and performing physical upgrades, such as RAM installations, alongside software support.

I drew directly on my software design and programming coursework from university to write automation scripts. Applying these concepts required adapting to ConnectWise Automate, which executes scripts through discrete logic steps rather than a traditional monolithic editor, as well as writing raw PowerShell and Shell scripts. While my previous customer service experience at Telus provided a solid foundation in communication and basic ticketing workflows, working on the internal IT side at Aurora gave me a practical understanding of enterprise system administration, tenant management, and user access policies.

One of the highlights of the term was visiting local production and network infrastructure facilities across Ontario, including sites in Bradford, Markham, and Brampton. Seeing the physical grow rooms, server closets, and automation systems put my daily desk work into perspective. Seeing employees using the equipment I had imaged and shipped out directly connected my technical tasks to the company's broader day-to-day operations.

Conclusions

This co-op term provided strong practical experience at the intersection of technical support, automation, and enterprise IT management. Working in the medicinal cannabis industry cleared away any preconceived stigmas; behind the specialized agricultural technology, Aurora operates with the scale, security requirements, and operational rigor of any global corporate enterprise. I thoroughly enjoyed solving practical technical problems, automating repetitive tasks to save staff time, and supporting employees across Canada and international offices in Australia, Europe, and Asia.

I would like to thank my manager, the IT Service Desk team, and my co-workers at Aurora Cannabis for their guidance, technical insights, and ongoing support throughout my work term.
